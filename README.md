## 🌄 About the Project

**Kyrgyz Tours** is a tourism platform focused on helping travelers discover Kyrgyzstan.

The project is being developed as a modern web experience where visitors can explore:

- 🏔️ Destinations across Kyrgyzstan
- 🗺️ Travel itineraries
- 🏨 Hotels and accommodation
- 🍽️ Restaurants and cafés
- 📸 Travel destinations and experiences
- ⭐ Traveler reviews
- 📍 Places and points of interest
- 🧭 Interactive maps and routes

The goal is to create a single platform where tourists can discover places, plan trips, and find useful information for traveling around Kyrgyzstan.

---

## ✨ Main Sections

The website currently includes the following main sections:

### 🏠 Home

The main landing section introducing Kyrgyzstan and the travel experience.

### ℹ️ About Us

Information about Kyrgyz Tours and the purpose of the platform.

### 🧭 Itineraries

Curated travel itineraries for different trip durations.

Current itinerary options include:

- 3 Days
- 4 Days
- 5 Days
- 14 Days

### 📸 Gallery

A visual collection of destinations, landscapes, and travel experiences in Kyrgyzstan.

### ⭐ Testimonials

Traveler reviews and experiences.

### 🗺️ Map

An interactive map planned for exploring places across Kyrgyzstan.

The map will eventually allow travelers to discover:

- Hotels
- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Attractions
- Activities
- Other points of interest

Additional filters will allow users to find places according to their needs, including dietary preferences such as:

- Vegan
- Vegetarian
- Halal
- Other food options

Route planning will also be integrated into the map experience.

### 📞 Contact Us

Contact information and communication options for travelers.
